FAQ

      
Straight answers.

      
The questions ops buyers usually ask before they send a sample.

      

        

          Do I need IT or a big software project?
          
No. You send a sample of the work that hurts. We build a private desk that matches your columns — not a platform you’ll spend months configuring.

        

        

          Will it match our Excel exactly?
          
That’s the point. ADDR lines, postcodes, phones, dates, status buckets — your headers and layout, not a generic template we force on you.

        

        

          Where does our data live?
          
Desks can run privately for your team, or we host them. The public demo uses fictional sites only. Production work uses your formats on the path you choose.

        

        

          How long until something useful is live?
          
Most first modules land in days to a couple of weeks once the sample and brief are clear. Full websites take longer — that’s why they’re priced separately.

        

        

          What if you can’t do what we asked?
          
No deposit until we agree the job is clear. If we can’t deliver it, you get a full refund of the deposit. Written into the offer.

        

        

          Desk or full website — which do I pick?
          
Pick a desk when the pain is Excel, reports, or delivery boards. Pick a full website when you need a complete public site. Same Basic · Gold · Premium visual steps on both paths.
